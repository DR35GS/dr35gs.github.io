02 / HOW I APPROACH IT

Measure first. Conclude carefully.

Hardware repair can be uncertain. A continuity beep or a resistance reading is evidence, but not necessarily proof that a component is faulty. Measurements need to be interpreted in circuit context and checked against the system's expected behaviour.

When I publish a repair or diagnostic log, I want to include the original symptom, measurement points, readings, changes made, and the final outcome—including cases where the device remains unrepaired.

NEXT STEP

Turn troubleshooting into case studies.

Add a separate page for each substantial repair with photos and confirmed results. Avoid publishing private customer details or presenting an unresolved diagnosis as a confirmed repair.
