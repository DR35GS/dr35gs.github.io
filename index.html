04 // RETROSPECTIVE

Course Reflection: SCST1133

Key Takeaways

Stepping into SCST1133 (Data Engineering Ecosystem) shifted how I view the lifecycle of information. Coming from a background heavy in hands-on hardware tinkering and foundational computer science, it is easy to fixate purely on the mechanics of execution. This course contextualized how raw data moves through an enterprise ecosystem—from ingestion and storage architecture to human-centered presentation via Design Thinking.

Areas for Improvement & Trajectory

Moving forward, my primary goal is to tighten the integration between my edge-computing projects and formal data engineering pipelines. While I am comfortable generating sensor data on an ESP32 or capturing landmark coordinates in a browser, I aim to deepen my proficiency in automated ETL orchestration, distributed database querying, and cloud-native warehousing to analyze that telemetry at scale.
